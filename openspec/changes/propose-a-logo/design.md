## Context

The plan's B8 describes the idea; the restyled web page fixed the palette. The owner picks from
proposals; the chosen one later replaces the web page's favicon and goes in the README.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** three proposals, each full colour, one colour and favicon; a page to compare them.

**Non-Goals:** the final logo; raster exports; a wordmark typeface (system fonts only).

## Decisions

### L1. Three readings of one idea

- **A, "On the line":** the case seen from the front, the lid's edge as the brow, big eyes on
  the lid, the mouth in the black base biting a cable that runs across, three packets draining
  into it.
- **B, "Count":** A with a wine-red cape's collar standing up behind the case, the classic
  vampire silhouette; amber eyes.
- **C, "Bite":** a minimal mark: the case as a rounded square head, two fangs sunk into an
  Ethernet plug below it; made to survive 16 px.

### L2. Drawn by hand as SVG, on a 128 grid

Plain shapes, no filters, no text in the marks, so they scale and stay small. The favicon forms
redraw on a 32 grid rather than scaling the full one down: at 16 px only the case, the eyes and
the fangs survive, so that is all they keep. The one-colour forms use a single ink with the
eyes and fangs cut out by a mask, so they work on light and dark.

Not taken: generating them with a tool (the shapes are few); tracing the owner's photos (they
show a trademark and are not the project's to publish).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **An agent's drawing is not a designer's.** → They are proposals; the owner may ask for a
  designer's pass on the chosen one.
