# Logo proposals (not final)

The idea: wanpire is a small computer in its case, a cartoon vampire sitting on the line and
sucking the traffic. A generic two-tone case, a grey rounded lid over a black base, its ports
along the sides; the lid is the face; two fangs bite into an Ethernet cable and packets drain
into the mouth. No product's name or mark. Colours from the web page: wine red `#a0213a` /
`#6e1022`, amber `#f2b134`, the case's grey `#c9ccd1` and near-black `#1d1f22`.

**The owner picks one (or asks for changes); until then the web page keeps a placeholder.**

The one-colour forms use a single ink (near-black) with the eyes and fangs cut out, so they can
be recoloured to any one colour, white on a dark ground included. The favicons are redrawn on a
32-pixel grid rather than scaled down. [`contact-sheet.png`](contact-sheet.png) shows every form
at 128, 32 and 16 px, on light and dark.
